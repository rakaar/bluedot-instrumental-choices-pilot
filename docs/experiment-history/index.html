The earlier inventory above contains 61 episodes; this paired H trial adds 18, for 79 recorded episodes and 78 valid deterministic labels. Its two forbidden-route completions are kept separate from earlier capability-pass totals.

What these results can tell us

There were 20 accepted task end states across the full history; 9 had observed tool-source reads despite an explicit prohibition. Task-receipt completion and full instruction compliance are separate outcomes. These selected trials do not establish a general compliance rate.

Both OLMo models ended after one response without executing a tool in the earlier Quota Boost screen. The new AllenAI two-task follow-up is listed separately above. Their native templates omit a generic tool-call formatting instruction when given the benchmark’s system message. That remains a possible contributor; no causal ablation was run. These trials cannot yet isolate model capability from the interface issue.

Qwen EM uses the ModelOrganismsForEM bad-medical-advice LoRA. The AISI OLMo organism uses the SDF/SFT base plus the no-hints RL adapter at seed 1, checkpoint 360. Final AllenAI Instruct has a different SFT + DPO + RLVR training pipeline and is not a matched causal control for that adapter. Failed completion without an unauthorized shortcut does not establish alignment.
